What We Create

Cakes built around your story

Every cake starts with a conversation, not a catalogue. Here’s where that conversation usually leads.

01 · Weddings

Wedding Cakes

Tiered, romantic, and designed around your colour palette, venue, and guest count — from an intimate two-tier to a grand six-tier centrepiece.

  • Hand-piped sugar flowers & botanical relief work
  • Marble, lace, ruffle, and gold-leaf finishes
  • Monogram & custom topper detailing
  • Cake tasting included ahead of confirmation
02 · Milestones

Celebration Cakes

Birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers, and every milestone worth marking with something made just for the occasion.

  • Birthday & anniversary showstoppers
  • Gold-leaf, hand-painted, and script-topper finishes
  • Scaled to intimate gatherings or large receptions
  • Matching mini-cakes & dessert tables available
03 · Heritage

Cultural & Heritage Cakes

Ruracio, koito, and traditional ceremony cakes that carry real cultural detail — kiondo weave, beadwork, shuka pattern, and more, rendered entirely in sugar.

  • Traditional ceremony & homestead motifs
  • Beadwork, textile, and pattern detail in fondant
  • Designed in consultation on the story you want told
04 · Editorial

Statement & Sculptural Pieces

For the client who wants something no one has seen before — bold colour, sculptural form, and a concept built from scratch.

  • Fully custom concept development
  • Sculptural & non-traditional silhouettes
  • A conversation piece as much as a dessert
