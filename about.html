The Designer

Meet Phyllis Kariuki

Phyllis Kariuki founded Cake Couture Kenya on a simple belief: a cake should be as considered as everything else on the table — the flowers, the dress, the venue. Today, Cake Couture Kenya is recognised as Kenya’s leading luxury wedding cake designer, known for bespoke tiers, meticulous sugarcraft, and a devoted following of over 18,000 on Instagram.

Every cake begins the same way: a real conversation about the couple, the ceremony, and the story behind it — then it’s built by hand, tier by tier, in the Karen atelier.

Philosophy

“Every tier has to earn its place.”

Cake Couture Kenya doesn’t work from a fixed catalogue. Colour, texture, and story come from the couple in front of us — whether that means hand-painted florals, gold-leaf relief, or a ceremony cake built around a family’s own traditions. Sugarcraft detail is the constant; everything else is designed around you.
